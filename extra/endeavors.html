Suupef Foods™ - SINGLE DOUBLE TOBACCO LLP

My First Business · October 26 – Present

This is the first high-risk thing I'm doing. I'll cut out the LinkedIn crap and tell you what it's really about. I wanted the name to be confusing to say, so I gave three names to the CA: Single AA Tobacco LLP, Double AA Tobacco LLP, and Single Double Tobacco LLP. The third one was selected.

Why tobacco in the name when I don't trade in tobacco? Before registering, I read a book about Aristotle Onassis and learned that tobacco is something that has demand even during a crisis. I was inspired. How did that motivation collapse? **BUDGET.** The budget required to procure in high quantities while keeping prices low was simply not there.

I actually tested various batches. I made American Dip and Swedish Snuff/Snus. I then tried giving them to people. Some happily tried them and gave valuable feedback, while others were scared because they had never seen anything like this before.

Then came the idea: Sweet Scented Supari – Areca Nut. I was actually able to replicate what's sold commercially, but again, the budget for bulk purchases wasn't there, and the process was too lengthy to carry out at home. It also produces a red colour that looks like a bloodstain on my white tiles. My mother won't spare me.

So, I'll sell mouth fresheners, either by procuring and packing them or by making them ourselves. That's the plan. I don't know whether it'll remain the same or change along the way. But for now, that's what I am going with.
