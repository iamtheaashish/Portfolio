Books

Books I've read and found the most valuable.

2026

  • Onassis, An Extravagant Life by Frank Brady

    Many people think of Aristotle Onassis' extravagant lifestyle, but few are aware of his hardships during wartime in his late teens or his strict work ethic. He was a tobacco trader, whaler, real-estate developer, and shipping magnate.

  • Straight A at Stanford and on to Harvard by Peter Rogers, MD Recommended

    I binge-listened to his YouTube videos during my evening walks. He is the best doctor in America who is still attractive at the age of 60. He has inspired me to adopt a 100% plant-based diet. This book is his academic-autobiography and strategies for succeeding in his career.

  • The Manipulated Man by Esther Vilar Recommended

    I watched a book review on YouTube by Peter Rogers MD. This book is almost like a troll, however, Esther throws true facts.

  • The Personal MBA: Master the Art of Business by Josh Kaufman

    Understanding how businesses function has two benefits for me. First, I'll be a better CRM & ERP developer. Second, I'll be a better entrepreneur in my life.
